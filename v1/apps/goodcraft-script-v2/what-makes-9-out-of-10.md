# What Makes This a 9/10?

**Current Score: 7.5/10**

---

## What's Holding It Back

| Dimension | Score | What's Missing |
|-----------|-------|----------------|
| User Acquisition | 6/10 | No proven distribution channel yet |
| Defensibility | 5/10 | No moat on day 1 |
| Financial Path | 7/10 | Market size is a guess |

---

## To Get to 9/10, You'd Need:

### 1. Validated Demand Before Building
A waitlist of 500+ Webflow developers who said "I need this" or a forum thread with 100 upvotes asking for exactly this. You built it because *you* needed it, which is good, but you haven't proven *others* will pay.

### 2. Distribution Advantage
If you had 10K Twitter followers who are Webflow developers, or an email list from Transform/Manage with proven engagement, or agency partnerships already lined up. Right now it's "I'll post in forums and hope."

### 3. Stronger Day-1 Moat
Proprietary data, exclusive partnerships, or network effects that exist at launch. The Blocks library *could* become a moat, but it's empty until users create content.

### 4. Pre-Validated Pricing
If 50 people had pre-paid or explicitly said "yes I'd pay $6/month for this." Right now it's assumed.

---

## The Honest Gap

You built a great product for a problem you have. That's the GoodCraft way and it's legit.

But a 9/10 idea has evidence that *enough other people* have the same problem and will pay to solve it. You're betting they exist. A 9/10 would already know.

---

## How to Close the Gap

- Launch and get 100 paying users → that's your proof
- If you hit $3K MRR in 6 months, this retroactively becomes a 9/10

The score isn't about the product quality — it's about the *certainty* of the opportunity. Right now there's a "will anyone show up?" uncertainty that only launching resolves.

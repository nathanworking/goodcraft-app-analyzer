## Competitive Landscape

### Direct Competitors (Webflow-Specific)

| Competitor | Threat Level | Key Difference |
|------------|--------------|----------------|
| Webflow Native Assets | Low | Basic; no optimization or CDN control |
| ImageOptim (manual) | Low | Not integrated; manual workflow |
| Figma to Webflow plugins | Low | Different workflow; not optimization-focused |

### Indirect Competitors (General Image CDNs)

| Competitor | Threat Level | Key Difference |
|------------|--------------|----------------|
| Cloudinary | Medium | Powerful but complex; not Webflow-native |
| ImageKit | Medium | Good value; requires manual integration |
| imgix | Medium | Enterprise-focused; overkill for most Webflow users |
| Bunny.net (direct) | Low | Transform uses it; adds Webflow integration value |

### Key Insight
General image CDNs are powerful but require manual setup. Transform's value is the **one-click Webflow integration**—optimization + CDN + deployment in a single workflow. No competitor offers this.

### Big Tech Risk: LOW-MEDIUM
Webflow could add image optimization natively. Mitigation: Transform offers more control, formats, and team features than Webflow would likely build.
